PRODUCT / TRUST AND PROVENANCE

Understand before you act.

Frequency Graph is an evidence-driven organizational investigation interface powered by FalkorDB. It is created by Val Rukhaylo / Altru.dev.

What runs today

Live property graph, deterministic bounded Cypher-backed investigation, multi-hop paths, hypothetical dependency simulations, and browser-local history.

What is not claimed

No autonomous agent, real action dispatch, independent action observation, production tenant isolation, or model-based reasoning. No external AI model or telemetry.

Privacy & ownership

Synthetic company fixture, read-only API. Browser history remains on your device. Public reference source is Apache-2.0; private DDC/Frequency implementations are excluded.

Reproducibility

Source, tests, architecture and technical terminology are public. FalkorDB keeps its original third-party license.
